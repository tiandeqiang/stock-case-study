### Case Overview

In 1993, Warren Buffett acquired Dexter Shoe for $433 million using Berkshire Hathaway stock.

### Core Pitfalls

1. **Misjudging the Economic Moat**: Low-cost imports destroyed domestic margins.
2. **Paying with Berkshire Stock**: The shares are now worth tens of billions of dollars.
